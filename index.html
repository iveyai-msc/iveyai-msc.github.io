> IVEY AI CLUB · MSc

IAI

AI is changing how analytics work gets done. IAI keeps Ivey MSc students current on the AI tools companies actually use, so we graduate ready to apply them and talk about them with confidence.

Join the club Follow @iveyai.msc

What we do

Build with AI. Learn from people who use it.

Our goal for year one: a core community of MSc students who can explain what an AI solution does, where it falls short, and what it is worth to a business.

Hackathons

Eight-hour weekend sprints, roughly every two months. Teams use AI tools to build a working solution to a real business problem, then pitch it to faculty and industry judges.

AI tool workshops

Hands-on sessions on new AI tools and how they're applied in business, run with the companies that build them or led by students.

Industry perspectives

Talks and Q&As with people working in machine learning, data science and AI product roles: how AI projects get scoped, sold and governed.
