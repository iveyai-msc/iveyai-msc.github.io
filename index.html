> IVEY AI CLUB · MSc

IAI

AI is changing how analytics work gets done. IAI keeps Ivey MSc students current on the AI tools companies actually use, so we graduate ready to apply them and talk about them with confidence.

Join the club We're hiring: apply Follow @iveyai.msc

What we do

Build with AI. Learn from people who use it.

Our goal for year one: a core community of MSc students who can explain what an AI solution does, where it falls short, and what it is worth to a business.

Hackathons

Team up and use AI tools to tackle a business problem together, then share what you built. Learn by building, not just watching.

AI tool workshops

Hands-on, beginner-friendly sessions on new AI tools and how businesses apply them. Bring a laptop, leave with a skill.

Industry perspectives

Conversations with people working with AI about what it looks like on the job: what works, what doesn't, and time for your questions.
